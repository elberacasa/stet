⏺ The switch is now a handover rather than a cut. index.html:598

  What happens between rounds, over 1.4s:
  - The fuse goes out first — the active tick stops pulsing before anything
  moves.
  - The row folds away right-to-left, staggered, while the clock and the header
  labels fade down. The clock holds at 00:00 instead of flashing the next
  round's time through the fade.
  - At 46% the screen crosses over — phase, round, colours, new fuse — while
  it's invisible.
  - The next round's ruler unrolls left-to-right and its clock fades up.

  The bloom was three 1.5s flashes over 4.5s. It's now a single slow swell
  peaking exactly on the cross-over, so the light both signals the round change
  and covers the one thing that can't fade (the rule lines changing colour).
