⏺ Empty repo — clean slate. Here's my design direction before I write it:

  Concept: an instrument, not a dashboard. Geometric sans numerals (Futura
  family, system-resident so it opens offline) against warm near-black, with a
  fuse — a ruler of hairline ticks, one per minute, that burns down left to
  right. The fuse's width is proportional to the phase length, so a break is
  literally a fifth as wide as a focus block. You can tell which phase you're in
  from across the room without reading a word. One accent, dusty apricot, and
  the amount of it on screen shrinks as you approach the break.

  The idea: the row of hairline ticks under the clock is a fuse — one tick per
  minute, every fifth one taller like a ruler. It burns left to right: spent
  minutes go dark, remaining minutes stay lit, so the amount of colour on screen
  shrinks as you approach the break. Because the tick pitch is fixed, a break
  is physically a fifth as wide as a focus block — you can tell which phase
  you're in from across the room without reading a word.

  Numerals are Futura (system-resident, so no webfont to load), each digit in
  its own fixed cell so nothing jitters as it counts. Labels are letterspaced
  mono — instrument panel, not dashboard. Paused dims the clock and stills the
  glow.

  When a round ends, four things fire at once so one of them reaches you: a
  synthesized bell (three inharmonic partials, warm — rising for the break,
  settling for the return to work), a slow bloom that lights the periphery of
  the screen and leaves the middle readable, the tab title, and a favicon ring
  that empties. Nothing is a beep.

  Space starts and pauses, R resets. Reset is a full reset — back to Focus,
  Round 01.

  What I verified in the browser: start/pause/resume/reset, the
  focus→break→focus rollover with the round counter incrementing, tick states
  and colours at each stage, no horizontal overflow, and the layout down to
  phone width. Two real bugs came out of it — spent ticks were stranded at the
  wrong colour, and the layout crowded at tablet width. Both fixed.

  What I couldn't verify: the actual sound. The Web Audio graph builds without
  error, but automation can't produce the user gesture that unlocks playback, so
  I never heard it. Press Start and tell me if the bell is too soft or too
  present — that's the one thing tuned blind.

  Two notes on the repo convention: I filed the background-tab constraint I hit
  as a stet note (Chrome freezes CSS transition timelines in an unfocused tab,
  which is why the ticks intentionally have none), and queued one stet ask on
  the accent colour — apricot vs celadon vs signal blue. It's non-blocking; the
  timer works as-is either way.
